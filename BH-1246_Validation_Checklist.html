Known Deviations

DEV-01: AAR_Record__c lookup targets UHN_AAR__c (not a AAR_Record__c object). Reason: BH-987 not yet delivered. Action: retarget field when BH-987 ships.
DEV-02: VR_Lock_After_Submit locks the full record after submission (not just the four specified fields). Reason: Salesforce ISPICKVAL requires a text literal as second arg; PRIORVALUE on a picklist cannot be compared field-to-field without enumerating all values. Accepted for V1. Revisit in V1.1.
DEV-03: Institute Director visibility granted via AAR_Institute_Leadership perm set ViewAll instead of criteria-based sharing rule. Reason: criteria-based sharing rules cannot reference Lookup fields. Institute-scoped filtering not enforced at record level in V1.
