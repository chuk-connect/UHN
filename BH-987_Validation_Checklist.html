Field length risk — Recognition_Name__c (30 chars) & Awarding_Agency__c (50 chars):
The spec specified Text(255) for both. Deployed lengths are significantly shorter. Real-world examples from the spec include "Order of Canada" (16 chars — fits) and "Royal Society of Canada" (23 chars — fits), but compound award names or full agency names could exceed 30 or 50 characters. Action required: Validate with business stakeholders that 30/50-char limits are acceptable, or increase to Text(255) before production.
